## Brand & Style
This design system embraces the refined precision, warmth, and clarity of Apple's Human Interface Guidelines for iOS health and fitness applications. It is tailored for wellness seekers, athletes, and clinical users who demand trustworthy, instantaneous telemetry presented with calm restraint rather than sensory overload.

The design movement centers on modern **Apple HIG Neoclassicism** combined with **Crisp Light Minimalism**:
- **Pure White Elevation on Grouped Slate:** Content sits within sculpted pure white cards against subtle system grey backgrounds (`#F2F2F7`), creating an instantly recognizable, native iOS spatial structure.
- **Airy Precision Telemetry:** Biometric feedback—heart rate, active kilocalories, recovery metrics, and workout splits—is presented through crisp typography, soft tinted badges, and glowing multi-ring activity charts.
- **Organic Humanism:** Generous 16px to 20px squircle contours, subtle 1px structural hairlines, and translucent blur headers create an atmosphere that feels friendly, medical-grade, and effortless.

## Colors
The palette balances the calm neutrality of iOS light surfaces with purposeful, high-chroma health telemetry accents. The primary structure is established through Apple system grays and pure whites, ensuring that vibrant metric indicators command immediate focus.

### Primary Color
- **`#007AFF` (System Blue):** Anchors core interactions, active tab elements, selection indicators, and primary navigational actions.

### Secondary & Tertiary Colors
- **`#34C759` (Activity Green):** Represents optimal recovery, exercise completion, cardio health, and positive trend deviations.
- **`#FF2D55` (Vitality Rose):** Designates active energy expenditure, movement targets, heart rate spikes, and critical metric milestones.

### Structural Neutrals
- **Background (`#F2F2F7`):** Native iOS system grouped background. Grounds all views in soft, low-contrast neutral warmth.
- **Card Background (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity pure white surfaces for cards, modular blocks, and telemetry pods.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E5E5EA`):** Subtle hairlines for structural separation and card perimeter refinement.
- **Typography Neutrals:** Primary text uses `#1C1C1E` (Onyx Slate, 95% opacity), secondary labels use `#8E8E93` (System Grey), and tertiary captions use `#AEAEB2`.

### Named Telemetry Hues
- **`activity-stand` (`#00C7BE`):** Aquatic cyan dedicated to hourly stand goals and respiratory rhythm.
- **`vitality-sleep` (`#5856D6`):** Deep indigo for circadian tracking, restorative stages, and time-in-bed metrics.
- **`vitality-heart` (`#FF3B30`):** High-alert pulse red for resting heart rate alerts, ECG baselines, and peak cardiovascular strain.

## Typography
Typographic hierarchy mirrors the geometric precision and clarity of Apple's system typeface (San Francisco), rendered through **Inter**. Strict optical balance, tightly controlled kerning, and numerical alignment maintain high-speed legibility across mobile screens and watch complications.

### Typographic Principles
- **Tabular Figures (`tnum`):** All telemetry readouts, step counts, calorie counters, and time deltas must be rendered with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to eliminate jitter when biometric streams update continuously.
- **Metric Tightening:** Primary metric values employ tight negative letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to mimic iOS Health header values, giving numbers a solid, deliberate presence.
- **Overline Category Caps:** Card category labels (e.g., `HEART RATE`, `SLEEP ANALYSIS`, `TRENDS`) utilize `label-caption` with uppercase transform and `letterSpacing: 0.04em` in muted slate (`#8E8E93`).
- **Hierarchy Rhythm:** Clear separation between `headline-metric` (the immediate raw number) and `body-sm` (the contextual unit or period, e.g., `BPM`, `KCAL`, `AVG 7 DAYS`).

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows iOS grouped table and modular dashboard paradigms. Content is presented in rounded card stacks against the grouped canvas, preserving an airy, uncluttered structure.

### Responsive Grid Model
- **Mobile (< 768px):** A single-column vertical stack with `margin-mobile` (16px) left and right safe margins. Modules sit as full-width pure white cards separated by an 12px to 16px vertical gap. Sub-metrics inside cards utilize a 2-column or 3-column micro-grid with 8px gutters.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 6-column fluid layout with 24px margins. Cards group into paired modules (2-up or 3-up) for high-level summaries like Activity Rings alongside Heart Rate Trends.
- **Desktop / Wide Telemetry (> 1024px):** 12-column fixed grid with a max container width of 1180px. Left rail (4 columns) displays daily telemetry totals and active rings; right master panel (8 columns) features expanded trend charts, split histograms, and recovery timelines.

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Card Padding:** Cards use `space-lg` (20px) internal padding on mobile and `space-xl` (24px) on tablet/desktop to provide ample breathing room around metrics.
- **Metric Stacking:** Metric number and accompanying unit label share a `space-xs` (4px) horizontal offset.
- **Section Headers:** Vertical distance between a grouped section title and its card collection is locked to `space-sm` (8px).

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation aligns with Apple's subtle ambient lighting model: zero heavy drops, zero aggressive shadows. Surfaces feel flat yet tactile, utilizing gentle tonal contrast, microscopic border framing, and translucent acrylic blurs.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** `#F2F2F7` (Apple system grouped background). Non-elevated structural foundation.
- **Level 1 (Default Metric Cards):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a micro hairline border of `1px solid #E5E5EA` and an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements & Action Sheets):** `#FFFFFF` with elevated shadow: `0 8px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)` and `1px solid rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`.
- **Navigation & Tab Bars:** Translucent pure white with Apple frosted glass blur: `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` accompanied by `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%)` and a bottom hairline border `#D1D1D6`.

### Glow Bleeds & Ring Highlights
Activity and vitality rings project a subtle, localized under-glow onto their immediate pure-white card container, simulating backlit hardware instrumentation:
- Move Ring: `filter: drop-shadow(0 4px 12px rgba(255, 45, 85, 0.25))`
- Exercise Ring: `filter: drop-shadow(0 4px 12px rgba(52, 199, 89, 0.25))`
- Stand Ring: `filter: drop-shadow(0 4px 12px rgba(0, 199, 190, 0.25))`

## Shapes
Shapes celebrate Apple's squircle geometry, delivering approachable, friendly contours that mirror iOS hardware hardware corners and Apple Watch enclosures.

### Geometry Hierarchy
- **Metric Cards & Modules:** 16px to 20px continuous corner radius (`rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl`), creating the quintessential iOS grouped card silhouette.
- **Buttons & Large Badges:** 12px to 14px radius (`rounded-md`) for secondary inputs, list cells, and modal dialogs.
- **Pills & Telemetry Badges:** Full circular capsules (`rounded-full`, 9999px) for activity tags, delta percentage chips, and category selectors.
- **Nested Inner Containers:** When placing sub-wells inside a 16px card, the inner container uses an 8px to 10px radius to preserve concentric curvature symmetry.

## Components

### Telemetry Activity Rings
- **Geometry:** Concentric circles or standalone gauge arcs with a stroke width of 10px to 14px.
- **Track Backing:** Inactive background tracks rendered in low-opacity tint (`rgba(accent, 0.15)`).
- **Stroke Terminus:** Active tracks feature fully rounded ends (`stroke-linecap: round`).
- **Glow & Gradient:** Subtly graduated color from ring base to tip, ending with an ambient drop-shadow (`drop-shadow(0 2px 8px rgba(accent, 0.35))`) at the leading edge.

### Metric Summary Cards
- **Structure:** Pure white card (`#FFFFFF`), 18px border-radius, hairline stroke `#E5E5EA`.
- **Top Row:** Left-aligned category icon and uppercase title (`label-caption` in `#8E8E93`), right-aligned timestamp or mini trend chevron.
- **Middle Section:** Prominent readout in `headline-metric` (`#1C1C1E`) flanked by unit text (e.g., `BPM` in `label-telemetry`).
- **Footer:** Crisp trend sparkline or mini comparison label (e.g., `+12% vs last week` tinted in `#34C759`).

### Status Badges & Trend Chips
- **Design:** Compact pill capsules (`h-6`, horizontal padding 10px).
- **Styling:** Low-saturation translucent background (`rgba(color, 0.10)`) with solid foreground text in corresponding system hue (`#007AFF`, `#34C759`, or `#FF2D55`).
- **Icons:** Inline 10px directional SF-style arrows (upward, downward, steady) preceding the delta value.

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill or 14px rounded rectangle. Solid System Blue (`#007AFF`) or System Onyx (`#1C1C1E`) fill with `#FFFFFF` text in weight 600. Tactile micro-scale feedback (`transform: scale(0.98)`) on active press.
- **Secondary / Tinted:** Soft background fill (`rgba(0, 122, 255, 0.10)`), text `#007AFF`, zero border.
- **Tertiary / Cell Button:** Borderless row item with primary text on left and right-aligned `#8E8E93` disclosure indicator chevron (`>`).

### Segmented Controls
- **Track:** Recessed system track in `#E5E5EA` with 8px radius and 2px inner inset padding.
- **Active Segment:** Elevated pure white chip (`#FFFFFF`) with 6px radius, backed by a soft shadow (`0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12)`).
- **Labels:** Centered `headline-card` text, transitioning smoothly between `#1C1C1E` (active) and `#8E8E93` (inactive).

### Telemetry Sparklines & Bar Histograms
- **Continuous Line Charts:** 2px smooth bezier stroke in vibrant health accent, resting over an airy vertical gradient fade (`from: rgba(accent, 0.15)` to `transparent`).
- **Hourly Bar Columns:** Pill-capped vertical rods in `#E5E5EA` for target goals, filled dynamically from base with system green or red to signal threshold attainment.
