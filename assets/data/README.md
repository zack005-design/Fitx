# Regional food catalog

`south_indian_foods.json` is the runtime catalog for foods associated with
Kerala, Tamil Nadu, and Karnataka. It is generated by
`tool/build_regional_food_catalog.py`.

## Nutrition fields

All nutrient values use 100 g of edible prepared food as their base. Energy is
in kcal; macro and saturated-fat values are in grams; cholesterol and minerals
are in milligrams; vitamin A and folate are in micrograms. Each entry also has a
household serving label and gram weight so the UI can calculate a serving.

`data_quality` distinguishes two kinds of values:

- `recipe_calculated`: copied from the Anuvaad Indian Nutrient Databank (INDB)
  2024.11 workbook, including its source code and extended nutrient panel.
- `estimated`: a regional household-recipe estimate. Recipes vary by household,
  oil, coconut, sugar, cut of meat, and cooked yield; these rows should never be
  presented as laboratory measurements. Unknown micronutrients are stored as
  `null`, not zero.

## Sources

- Indian Nutrient Databank: https://www.anuvaad.org.in/indian-nutrient-databank/
- INDB methodology: https://www.anuvaad.org.in/methodology/
- ICMR-NIN Indian Food Composition Tables 2017:
  https://www.nin.res.in/ebooks/IFCT2017.pdf

INDB calculates cooked recipes primarily from ICMR-NIN IFCT 2017/2004, with
supplementary UK CoFID and USDA FoodData Central ingredient values. The app
keeps the INDB food code on every matched row for auditability.

No finite catalog can literally contain every household or restaurant recipe
from three states. Additions should use a reproducible recipe and cooked yield,
retain provenance, and be reviewed by a qualified nutrition professional before
the data is used for clinical or medical decisions.
